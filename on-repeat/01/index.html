The celebrations, people, and moments that made 2026 unforgettable. Plus, what’s next.

What a year.

From museums and ballrooms to barns, park pavilions, and even a botanical garden in Louisville, 2026 took us to some pretty incredible places.

But looking back, it’s the moments that stand out most.

A couple sharing one last dance after everyone had left the room. A live drummer bringing a whole different energy to the night. Dance floors that stayed packed until the lights came on. And so many people we got to know along the way.

This was our biggest year yet at Ife Luxe Collective, and we’re grateful to everyone who played a part in it. To our couples who trusted us with their celebrations, and the planners, venues, and creative partners we got to work alongside, thank you.

We’re already looking forward to what’s coming in 2027, but there are a few moments from this year worth playing back first.

Here’s a little of what we’ve been up to, what’s coming next, and some of the people who make this all worth doing.

Three nights we're still talking about

From the Booth

Kaitlyn & Kyle

Oct 3 · Mojave East

 

October 3rd. Mean Girls Day. And Kaitlyn and Kyle understood the assignment, right down to the marquee sign.

They even surprised John with a tip in the form of sneakers in his favorite color. Somewhere along the way, these two went from clients to friends.

But one of the best moments came at the very end. With their guests outside, John played one last song just for the two of them on an empty dance floor.

No crowd, no distractions. Just the two of them enjoying one final moment together.

Alex & Damien

Aug 28 · The Palomar

 

Alex and Damien brought so much personality to their celebration. With Alex’s Puerto Rican roots and a shared vision for a night that felt completely their own, there was nothing ordinary about this wedding.

Their first dance was surrounded by clouds, a live drummer brought a whole new level of energy playing alongside John, and custom turntable platters were designed specifically for their wedding.

Between the music, the personal touches, and the energy in the room, this was one of those nights that reminded us why we love what we do.

Kylee & Tyler

Oct 2 · Ault Park

 

Kylee and Tyler didn’t just plan a wedding. They created an entire experience around becoming The Cords, Est. 2026.

Custom magazines telling their love story, matching hats at their very own merch stand, and little details that made the entire day feel like them.

And when it was time to party? That dance floor stayed full until the lights came on. Nobody was ready for this one to end.
